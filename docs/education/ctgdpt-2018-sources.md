# HH School curriculum and rights register

HH School uses official programme documents only to identify the structure and
learning outcomes of the Vietnamese general education programme. It does not
copy textbooks, workbook questions, commercial test banks or publisher media.

These references are marked `REFERENCE_ONLY`. They are not treated as open
licensed source content.

UNESCO's 2019 OER Recommendation is the rights-policy reference used by this
project: public-domain or openly licensed materials must allow no-cost access,
reuse, repurposing, adaptation and redistribution:
https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-open-educational-resources-oer

## Initial content

The lesson and question samples in `hh-school-curriculum.js` are original HH
School examples. Their editorial state is explicitly `checked`; none is
represented as approved by the Ministry or as an
official school grade.

External learning material must follow:

`discovery → quarantine → licence evidence → content review → human approval → publication`

Allowed candidates are verified public-domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA or another
clear open licence compatible with the intended use. “Free”, Google image
results, YouTube videos and user-uploaded archives are not evidence of reuse
permission.
