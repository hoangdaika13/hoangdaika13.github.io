# JMdict vocabulary notice

`japanese-vocabulary-10k.js` contains a compact selection derived from JMdict English Common.

- Upstream: Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG) JMdict.
- JSON conversion: `scriptin/jmdict-simplified`.
- Dictionary version: 3.6.2, dated 2026-07-27.
- Source archive: `jmdict-eng-common-3.6.2+20260727141257.json.zip`.
- Source SHA-256: `e31349d93c733a8b61c8e183dc17c6fef373a8b0373c7ff18cd5559f4670bc26`.
- License: https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html
- Generated entries: 9,640 English-gloss entries, combined at runtime with 360 HH Vietnamese/thematic entries for exactly 10,000 vocabulary items.

The generated pack removes names and entries tagged vulgar, derogatory, archaic, obsolete, rare, dated, sensitive, or X-rated. This filtering is an educational product decision and does not change the upstream dictionary.
